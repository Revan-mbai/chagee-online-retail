First improvement: quote the real wait, then send overflow to a quieter outlet nearby. A toy lunch rush in 10-minute slots; every figure is illustrative.

Click Today. Orders keep landing in full slots, in red. The app still promises 15 minutes, the real wait reaches 40, and five customers cancel, like the one on the phone.

Click With AI. A full slot triggers an alert: an honest next slot, or Outlet B, ranked by ready time and walk. Sixteen orders move into B's spare capacity: 52 of 53 kept, four more than today. B's staff and rent are already paid, so most of each rerouted cup is profit.

Benefit: peak is 40% of volume. 40% × 3% more peak cups × S$48.2M sales base (44 outlets × about 500 cups a day × S$6 × 365) ≈ S$0.58M revenue; at 70% margin, about S$0.4M gross profit a year.

Second improvement: discount only when there is room to fill. The menu price never changes; only the voucher moves, capped at S$1. Off-peak is the default: demand sits under capacity, so the app offers S$1 off our highest-profit drink.

Click Peak: over capacity, so no voucher, S$0.50 off pre-orders made before the rush, and fewer low-profit Grab orders. Click Hot day: demand is already high, so only S$0.50. Click Rain: S$1 on app delivery or sheltered pick-up. Click Event: no discount, push pre-orders. Click Quiet CBD: S$1 fills weekend capacity. Click Dormant: two nudges, and a holdout group measures the lift.

Click Off-peak again: each voucher cup still earns S$3.20, S$6 minus S$1 minus S$1.80, because staff and rent are already paid. Delivery is 25% of S$48.2M, so S$12.05M; 5% more at about 40% margin is about S$241k profit a year. Illustrative figures.

Finally: get customers to collect their own orders.

Click GrabFood delivery: an 18 to 30% commission, about S$1.10 to S$1.80 of a S$6 cup, goes to Grab; the ice melts while it waits for a rider; and Grab keeps the customer data. Click In-app delivery: Chagee keeps the customer but still pays a rider fee on every order (amount not given; the grey cap is only a marker). Click App pick-up: no commission, no rider, a fresh drink, and the customer is ours. The strip lists five incentives, such as a free upsize for 5 pick-ups in a month.

Point to the red card: none of this works until pick-up waits are short and accurate; AI ready-time prediction makes the quoted time accurate.

Arithmetic: S$48.2M sales × 25% delivery = S$12.05M; 10% shifting is S$1.2M; at a 17% average saving per shifted order, about S$205k a year. All figures are illustrative.
