Second improvement: discount only when the outlet has room to fill. The menu price stays at S$6; only the voucher moves. The slide opens at 3 pm on a normal day, well under capacity, so the app offers S$1 off our highest-profit drink.

Press Play to run the day: an hour before lunch, pre-orders get S$0.50 off; at the peak, vouchers stop and we take fewer Grab orders; then the S$1 returns. Click Hot: the voucher shrinks to S$0.50. Rain: the S$1 moves to delivery or sheltered pick-up. Event nearby, such as a concert or F1: no discount, push pre-orders.

Open Dormant members and click through the steps: trigger, two nudges and the holdout.

Close on the per-cup card: S$6 minus S$1 minus S$1.80 still earns S$3.20, about S$241k a year. All figures are illustrative.
