Second improvement: discount only when there is room to fill. The menu price never changes; only the voucher moves, capped at S$1. Off-peak is the default: demand sits under capacity, so the app offers S$1 off our highest-profit drink.

Click Peak: over capacity, so no voucher, S$0.50 off pre-orders made before the rush, and fewer low-profit Grab orders. Click Hot day: demand is already high, so only S$0.50. Click Rain: S$1 on app delivery or sheltered pick-up. Click Event: no discount, push pre-orders. Click Quiet CBD: S$1 fills weekend capacity. Click Dormant: two nudges, and a holdout group measures the lift.

Click Off-peak again: each voucher cup still earns S$3.20, S$6 minus S$1 minus S$1.80, because staff and rent are already paid. Delivery is 25% of S$48.2M, so S$12.05M; 5% more at about 40% margin is about S$241k profit a year. Illustrative figures.
