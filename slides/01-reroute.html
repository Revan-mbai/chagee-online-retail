First improvement: quote the real wait, then send overflow to a quieter outlet nearby. A toy lunch rush in 10-minute slots; every figure is illustrative.

Outlet A's slots fill from 12:20. Instead of letting orders pile up, the agent reads live queues at both outlets, learns from past decisions, and recommends how to balance them. Sixteen orders move into Outlet B's spare capacity: 52 of 53 kept, four more than today, and only one customer walks away because of the wait.

The customer sees one of two things. Option A: a pop-up in the Chagee app tells pick-up customers the real wait and suggests the outlet with room, with a switch perk; the customer chooses before paying. Option B: the busy outlet stops taking orders on delivery apps so riders go next door; the zone manager approves the pause.

Benefit: peak is 40% of volume. 40% × 3% more peak cups × S$48.2M sales base (44 outlets × about 500 cups a day × S$6 × 365) ≈ S$0.58M revenue; at 70% margin, about S$0.4M gross profit a year.
