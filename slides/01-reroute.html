First improvement: quote the real wait, then send overflow to a quieter outlet nearby. A toy lunch rush in 10-minute slots; every figure is illustrative.

Click Today. Orders keep landing in full slots, in red. The app still promises 15 minutes, the real wait reaches 40, and five customers cancel, like the one on the phone.

Click With AI. A full slot triggers an alert: an honest next slot, or Outlet B, ranked by ready time and walk. Sixteen orders move into B's spare capacity: 52 of 53 kept, four more than today. B's staff and rent are already paid, so most of each rerouted cup is profit.

Benefit: peak is 40% of volume. 40% × 3% more peak cups × S$48.2M sales base (44 outlets × about 500 cups a day × S$6 × 365) ≈ S$0.58M revenue; at 70% margin, about S$0.4M gross profit a year.
