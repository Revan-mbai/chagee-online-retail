Finally: get customers to collect their own orders.

Click GrabFood delivery: an 18 to 30% commission, about S$1.10 to S$1.80 of a S$6 cup, goes to Grab; the ice melts while it waits for a rider; and Grab keeps the customer data. Click In-app delivery: Chagee keeps the customer but still pays a rider fee on every order (amount not given; the grey cap is only a marker). Click App pick-up: no commission, no rider, a fresh drink, and the customer is ours. The strip lists five incentives, such as a free upsize for 5 pick-ups in a month.

Point to the red card: none of this works until pick-up waits are short and accurate; AI ready-time prediction makes the quoted time accurate.

Arithmetic: S$48.2M sales × 25% delivery = S$12.05M; 10% shifting is S$1.2M; at a 17% average saving per shifted order, about S$205k a year. All figures are illustrative.
